About the shop

A neighbourhood jeweller you can walk up to

I opened Devansh Jewellery at Indira Chauraha, Mahroni five years ago. I named it after my son, Devansh, because I wanted a shop he could one day be proud of.

Most of our customers are families we now know by name. They come for a wedding set, stay for chai, and come back for the next festival. I would rather earn that trust than win a quick sale.

Whatever you buy here, ask questions. Show me what you have in mind, and I will tell you honestly what suits your budget.
